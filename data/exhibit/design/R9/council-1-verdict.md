# Verdict of the Design Council
## The Ground Keeps Moving, display redesign

## Where the council agrees

**The design already exists in the convergence.** Five of seven concepts independently arrived at the same four primitives. A persistent map of Hyde Park that accumulates marks and never erases them. A five-instrument strip that makes the overlap of 1921 to 1970 permanently visible. A running ledger of dollars and people from 1833 to 2026. A true-scale wealth climb where scroll distance is the axis. First-principles named this plainly in review, and the council accepts it. These four ARE the exhibit. The decision before us was packaging, order, and discipline, not invention.

**The turntable dies, the geometry lives.** Every advisor kept the 703-polygon HOLC dataset with its joined surveyor sheets and every advisor killed the 2.5D relief turntable. Orbiting a map is the grammar of the demo the owner already rejected, and a thumb has no reason to orbit anything.

**Nothing from the current presentation survives.** The label-block and evidence-station document flow carries zero framing forward. Unanimous.

**The bombing chapter is settled.** No motion, no counters, no gamification, no imagery of victims. Information-designer's Empty Column, a real HTML table of forty rows whose Outcome cell is rendered, bordered, and blank in every row, closed by a totals row reading bombings 58, killed 2, convictions 0, was named steal-worthy by five of six peer reviewers. It ships verbatim. The chapter gets a standalone owner sign-off against curator's descendant test, separate from the rest of launch.

**The engineering laws are unanimous.** The reduced-motion build, the keyboard build, and the phone build are the same build of discrete resolved states (art-director). No tween may display a value that does not resolve to a dated fact ID (art-director). Performance is CI acceptance criteria, not aspiration, with LCP under 2.0s on a throttled Moto G profile (phone-executor). Native scroll is never hijacked. The complete argument must read start to finish with zero interactions, contrarian's projector-off test.

**The palette is paper and ink.** Cream ground, ink text, the existing site families. The four HOLC colors are reserved exclusively for grades so color stays an honest encoding, and rust is reserved for present-day consequence so the visitor is trained across twelve chapters to read rust as now. No new design system. The owner has rejected one once.

**Motion-3d's Sediment is the trap.** Six of seven reviewers, including three who admire its bombing chapter, named it the council's biggest blind spot. The council agrees it is the killed game reincarnated with better vocabulary.

## Where the council clashes

**Zero motion versus witnessed change.** Contrarian bans all scroll-linked behavior and builds on accumulation alone. Motion-3d's counter is the sharpest sentence in the reviews, that at 390px the Overprint quietly rebuilds the exact artifact the owner rejected, a long static column a thumb flicks past in ninety seconds, and that covenants spreading and grades flooding are not gimmicks but the argument performed. The council sides with witnessed change executed as stepped, sourced states, while adopting contrarian's discipline wholesale, the written motion budget, the projector-off floor, and the week-one 390px gate on the thesis graphic.

**How to protect the bombing chapter from scroll-scrubbers.** First-principles suspends scroll-time entirely and requires a tap to continue. Contrarian praised this as the only structural protection proposed. Art-director flagged it as a hard accessibility and bounce hazard that fights native scroll. The council sides with art-director. We protect the chapter with register instead of mechanics, a hard cut in (motion-3d), the page's transition system unbound across that DOM range, a full viewport of dark air before and after (curator), and forty rows the thumb must pass one by one.

**Dark gallery versus site identity.** Curator's room-by-room light values are genuine museum craft, but the near-black gallery diverges from the site's cream identity, and this owner has already killed one visual overhaul. The council keeps the cream register and steals curator's pacing idea at small scale, one value dip for the bombing chapter and one warm shift for the church basement.

**Chronology purity versus stating the charge first.** Every concept banked the 2026 payoff at the bottom of a ten-plus-minute scroll. Three reviewers independently attacked this. The council sides with the minority prosecutor view, state the charge and the damages in the first ninety seconds, then let the chronology be the proof.

**Whose chassis wins.** Stripped of self-votes, only two concepts earned external endorsements, phone-executor (endorsed by contrarian) and information-designer (endorsed by first-principles). The council builds on both, phone-executor as the engineering chassis and build plan, information-designer as the encoding system.

## Blind spots the council caught

- **The fifty-year gap.** Contrarian caught that every concept vaults from 1971 to a 2022 wealth figure, which is exactly where a skeptical professor or museum board will attack. Magnitude without causation is where engineered quietly becomes a big number happened. The chosen direction stages a sourced bridge between the CBL and the climb, contingent on what the registry holds (see open questions).
- **The visitor who leaves at chapter 3.** First-principles, motion-3d, and phone-executor all caught that no concept designs the abandonment path, the deep link, the refresh, or the returning visitor. The chosen direction requires the charge inside ninety seconds, a takeaway sentence closing every act, stable chapter anchor URLs, and all persistent chrome derived as a pure function of scroll position so any entry point renders a coherent scene.
- **People demoted to marginalia.** Curator and art-director both caught that every concept made paperwork the protagonist and reduced nine named human beings to pinned quotes. The chosen direction gives one room to people, the Contract Buyers League chapter is carried by Ruth Wells and Clyde Ross with the paperwork demoted to their evidence, and every voice stands at its real address and date in the flow.
- **The climb is felt but never seen.** Information-designer caught that all seven concepts let the visitor traverse the $285,010 bar serially without ever holding both bars in one view. Fixed with a persistent true-scale minimap during the climb and a summit pull-back frame.
- **Nominal versus real dollars.** Information-designer caught that an 1833 treaty figure and a 2022 Fed figure in one running ledger imply a summable series. The ledger must render its dollar convention as part of the design.
- **Nobody gates on feel.** Contrarian caught that the last two builds died on how they felt, yet every concept gates only on technical prototypes. The build plan adds an owner kill-check on one fully finished chapter before the remaining twelve are built.
- **Still moving is asserted, not shown.** Motion-3d caught that every finale stops at a receipt dated 2022. The bridge section and the grade receipt are the answer we can source today; the researcher owes us whatever present-tense instrument the registry can support.

## The chosen direction

**The direction is The Same Map (phone-executor's chassis) carrying The Plate and the Register's encodings (information-designer), opened with the erase-and-rebuild contract (first-principles), paced and humanized by the Curator, governed by the Art-Director's laws, and gated by the Contrarian's discipline.** One map loads before anything else and never leaves the screen. Thirteen chapters do things to it. The charge is stated in the first ninety seconds and the chronology proves it. The bill is paid at the end in scroll distance.

### The through-line graphic system

Three persistent objects plus one hairline, all visible together at 390px, all derived purely from scroll position so deep links and refreshes reconstruct them.

1. **The Stage** (phone-executor, art-director, first-principles). A sticky top pane of roughly 50svh holding the flat 1940 map as inline SVG, simplified offline to under 80KB gzipped, one merged path per grade for fills, a per-polygon hit layer mounted only in chapters that invite tapping. 2. **The Instrument Register** (information-designer, endorsed by curator). Five horizontal bars on one 1900 to 2026 axis with true start and end dates, the realtors' rule, covenants, HOLC/FHA, urban renewal, contract selling. It appears docked beneath the Stage from the cold open as a 24px strip with a riding date cursor showing which instruments are live at the current chapter, expands full-screen at the chapter 4 overture with handoff annotations at the joints, and each bar is a tappable jump link to its chapter (contrarian). Court defeats terminate bars with dated annotations such as unenforceable 1948 (first-principles' overstamp, flattened), but the strip is never stamped defeated wholesale, because the ground is still moving (art-director's correction). This graphic is the thesis and is the most carefully typeset object on the page.
3. **The Ledger Rail** (information-designer, phone-executor). A 24px band at the bottom edge that posts the 11 dated dollars-and-people entries as the story reaches them, set in tabular figures, rust because rust means now, carrying a visible dollar-convention note. It expands into the finale.
4. **The Spine** (curator, endorsed by three reviewers). A 3px rule at the left screen edge doubling as scroll progress. It begins as black surveyor's ink at 1832 and turns permanently HOLC D-red at the exact scroll position where the federal letterhead appears in chapter 8. It ends at the visitor's own grade receipt.

Chrome budget outside the Stage is roughly 51px total. A week-one test at 390px with all chrome present is a build gate; if it reads as clutter, the Register absorbs the Ledger Rail into one strip before anything else is built.

### Art direction

Paper and ink in the existing site tokens and families, no new design system. The pigment law is absolute, the four HOLC colors mean grades and nothing else, rust means present-day consequence and the one CTA, the D-red of the Spine is a grade meaning by design. Display serif for datelines and chapter titles, body face for cards capped at 50 words, tabular figures mandatory in the docket, the Ledger, and every axis, and a document face for machine-produced text that reads as typescript without cosplaying a typewriter (art-director). Texture comes only from the real scans; no synthetic grain anywhere. Light is flat and even except two designed value shifts, down for the bombing chapter, warm for the basement (curator, miniaturized). The fifteen archival photographs are the only halftone on the page, run full-column with credits, nothing overlaid.

### Mobile strategy

Phone-executor's contract, adopted as CI acceptance criteria. Designed at 390px and adapted up, nothing exists on desktop that does not exist on the phone. Sticky Stage in svh units with a no-sticky inline fallback behind a feature check. All chapter transitions fire on discrete IntersectionObserver thresholds, never per-scroll-pixel. Initial route JS under 180KB gzipped, the map inline SVG under 80KB gzipped, LCP under 2.0s and TBT under 200ms on a throttled Moto G profile, measured by Lighthouse in CI on every push. Images lazy AVIF with reserved dimensions and blur placeholders, sheet scans loaded at tap time, the reading room code-split. Canvas only for the family grid. No WebGL, no three.js, no audio. Desktop moves the Stage to a left column.

### Interaction model

Two gestures, learned in Act 0 and never changed (information-designer). ADVANCE is scroll, which always means time moving across the Stage. INSPECT is tap, and every on-screen number, mark, polygon, and document carries a fact chip opening its registry source in a bottom sheet, so verification is a thumb gesture. One labeled tap opportunity per chapter, one native slider on the whole page, two acts of deliberate input at the end, location and the answer wall. Zero interactions still delivers the complete argument (contrarian's floor). Keyboard users step the identical resolved states with visible focus and skip links; screen readers get the docket and Ledger as real tables, polygons as named buttons, and a prose equivalent per act. Every chapter is a stable anchor URL and every piece of persistent chrome is a pure function of scroll offset, so shared links, refreshes, and the back button always render a coherent scene.

### Build order and gates

Week one builds and phone-tests the Instrument Register at 390px and the Stage's jank profile on a real mid-range Android; either failing kills or reshapes the concept before sunk cost (contrarian, phone-executor). Week two finishes one complete chapter, Act 3's paperwork sequence, to final polish for an owner kill-check on how it feels, because feel is what killed the last two builds (contrarian's process catch). The bombing chapter gets its separate descendant-test sign-off. A written motion budget is enforced in code review, and the copy checklist runs as a build gate across all new microcopy, not as a post-hoc pass. Roughly six weeks, one developer, with the turntable, all 3D, and audio explicitly out of scope to protect it.

## What we deliberately reject and why

- **Motion-3d's Sediment, wholesale.** The scroll-driven camera, the delamination, the raking-light open, and the 24-plate prerender pipeline rebuild the grammar of the killed game, cost up to 6MB on a page with a mid-range-phone constraint, create a permanent asset-pipeline tax, and stage D-graded Black neighborhoods as theatrical shadow before the visitor knows anything. Its core-sample finale draws qualitative strata at a reconciled dollar scale, a graphic that lies with a label that apologizes, disqualifying on a page whose credibility is its sourcing. We keep exactly one of its moves, the hard cut into the bombing chapter.
- **Contrarian's zero-motion absolutism.** At 390px the Overprint collapses back into a static column the owner already scrolls past, and it argues the owner out of his own brief. We keep its discipline and reject its crouch. The per-chapter overprint plates are also redundant on a page whose Stage already accumulates; the finale's all-layers view with legend toggles delivers that payoff once.
- **Curator's dark gallery palette.** The room craft is right, the identity break is wrong for an owner who has already rejected one visual overhaul. Stolen at small scale instead.
- **First-principles' suspended-time gate at the bombing chapter.** Structurally noble, but it hijacks native scroll, which the council's own engineering law bans. Register break and air do the work instead.
- **The stamp tray, brass plinths, wax seals, and drawer furniture.** Skeuomorphic chrome that decays into cuteness. The Register is the same argument as an honest Gantt, and its bars are the jump links.
- **Any all-instruments-defeated finale.** Stamping all five broken visually declares the machine dead, which contradicts the exhibit's closing claim. Bars terminate with dated annotations; the strip runs to 2026.
- **The 2.5D relief turntable and the document-flow label blocks.** Unanimous, and the latter is the thing the owner rejected.

## Open questions for the researcher

1. **The 1971 to 2022 bridge.** What do the registry and the 11-entry ledger actually hold between 1971 and 2022, and can a sourced bridge section be staged (compounded denied equity, appraisal gaps, later lending patterns on the same footprint)? If the registry cannot support it, what is the honest caption for the climb that pre-empts the two-generations-ago objection?
2. **Covenant date density.** Does the registry hold per-tract or per-year covenant adoption data, or must the hatch layer ship as two honest states, pre-1927 and peak coverage?
3. **Grade flood order.** Is the HOLC survey sequence documented well enough to source the re-inking order, or should the flood step by grade with a caption saying the order is a design choice, not history?
4. **Ledger dollar convention.** Dollars of their year with a stated non-comparability rule, or a deflator, and what does each entry's source permit?
5. **Clearance sequence.** Is a dated demolition order for the 638 footprints sourced, or is before-and-after the honest maximum?
6. **Memorial typography precedent.** How do the Legacy Museum and comparable institutions handle naming hierarchy and record typography for events like the bombing years, and what exact screen-reader wording for the empty outcome cell is both accurate to the registry and respectful to descendants?
7. **The eight unplaced incidents.** What on-page language explains 40 docket rows against 32 mapped marks so the discrepancy reads as archival honesty rather than error?
8. **Abandonment benchmarks.** What do current scrollytelling completion data say about act length and about whether a ninety-second charge measurably changes what early-exit visitors retain, and what lightweight test protocol can verify ours before launch?
9. **iOS Safari in 2026.** Current behavior of sticky plus svh under address-bar collapse, and the correct trigger conditions for the no-sticky fallback layout.
10. **The shareable unit.** Should the grade receipt card generate a shareable image, and what are the privacy implications of sharing a location-derived artifact; likewise whether chapter anchors warrant per-chapter OG images so screenshots travel with their sourcing.
