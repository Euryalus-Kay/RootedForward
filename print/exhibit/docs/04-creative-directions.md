# Phase 4. Three directions, one choice

Three genuinely different graphic ideas were developed against the
same content architecture. Each could carry the show. They differ in
what the visitor's eye is given first, a document, a photograph, or a
map.

---

## Direction A · The Survey File

**Idea.** The exhibition presents itself as the neighborhood's own
paper record, opened flat and annotated. Every panel is a numbered
sheet in one civic file. The ground is plat-book linen, the
structure is carbon ink rule-work, and the twentieth century's
documents (a code of ethics, a covenant, a federal map, a ledger)
appear as evidence at readable size, with the damning phrases set
large rather than circled. One semantic red is reserved for
extraction and exclusion, so when red appears it means something.

**Why Hyde Park.** The instruments that segregated this neighborhood
were literally paper. A pledge, a code, a deed clause, a survey map,
an installment contract. A paper-led system is not a style here, it
is the subject. It also extends the owner's built exhibit system
(linen, carbon, HOLC blue, appraisal gold, semantic red) so the
physical and digital shows are one program.

**Type.** Source Serif 4 for display and text (the org's own face,
which doubles convincingly as a bookish document face). Archivo
Narrow, caps, letterspaced, for the plat-sheet apparatus (sheet
numbers, era ranges, section labels, map annotation). IBM Plex Mono
for ledger figures only. Two working families plus a figures-only
accent.

**Color.** Linen `#EDE6D6` field, deep linen `#E2D8C2` for document
wells, carbon `#1C1A17` type and rules, HOLC blue `#4A6B8A` and
appraisal gold `#C9A227` as archival accents inside maps and grades,
red `#B0322B` strictly semantic. Forest and rust survive only in the
organization signature block, so the org mark reads as the publisher
of the file, not its subject.

**Images.** Duotone-free. Archival photographs print as themselves,
slightly warmed, squared to the grid, with one dominant image per
panel at 25 to 40 percent of the sheet. Documents are shown as
objects on the linen (butted to hairline rules, never drop-shadowed,
never rotated-and-taped, no fake paper texture).

**Maps.** Redrawn, not screenshotted. The HOLC sheet redrawn from
the government's polygons in the survey's own four grades. The walk
route drawn from the app's stop coordinates over a fine street grid
in carbon, route in red because the route traces the harm.

**Timeline.** No circles on a string. A ruled era band, like the
edge of a plat sheet, with years set in Narrow caps and one-line
entries. The running ledger foot (debits and credits per era, in
Plex Mono) is the series' connective tissue across all four sheets.

**Grid.** Six columns on 36×48 (1.5in margins, 0.4in gutters), a
40pt baseline. Banners run the same six columns at 33in with the
composition rebuilt for standing eye height.

**Continuity.** Identical masthead logic (sheet number, era, running
title), identical caption and credit conventions, different lead
object per sheet. Sheet 1 photograph-led, Sheet 2 document-led,
Sheet 3 ledger-led, Sheet 4 photograph-and-map-led.

**Strengths.** Specific to the subject, unmistakably Rooted Forward,
cheap to print faithfully on matte board (linen field, no heavy ink
coverage), and it survives bad lighting. The semantic red gives the
series a visible ethic.

**Risks.** If the rule-work is timid it drifts toward "nice
handout". Needs courage in scale (the title at 150pt, the map at
28 inches, the number 4,000 at eight inches). If documents are
over-cropped it becomes a scrapbook. Mitigation, one dominant
object per sheet, everything else subordinated.

## Direction B · The Dark Ground

**Idea.** Four near-black panels (deep forest-ink), full-bleed
archival photography, cream typography, in the register of the
NMAAHC history galleries. Quotes at architectural scale. Documents
appear as bright artifacts floating on the dark field.

**Why Hyde Park.** The subject is grave, and a dark ground carries
grief and scale with authority. The night-lit White City image was
born for a black field.

**Type.** Source Serif display in cream, DM Sans apparatus.
**Color.** Ink `#141a17` field, cream text, rust rules, no other color.
**Images.** Large, full-bleed, slightly lifted blacks.
**Maps.** Cream linework knocked out of the dark.
**Timeline.** Light-on-dark era band.

**Strengths.** Immediate drama at fifteen feet. Portfolio-grade
presence. Strong hierarchy almost for free.

**Risks.** The four strongest images in the show are a
chromolithograph, an 1871 engraved plan, a colored survey map, and a
ledger. Three of the four are paper objects that die on black.
Heavy ink coverage scuffs on foam board and bands on cheap vinyl.
Four dark monoliths also read as one long funeral, while the tour's
own voice is daylight, plain, and specific. And it borrows NMAAHC's
authority rather than earning Rooted Forward's own.

---

## Direction C · The Plat Grid

**Idea.** The Hyde Park street grid is the chassis. Every panel is
organized on an underlying cadastral drawing of the neighborhood,
with columns hung on section-line streets and content anchored to
the places where it happened. The exhibition reads as one continuous
map sliced into four sheets.

**Why Hyde Park.** The story is geographic. Boulevards for the
white face of the resort, the tracks for its Black workforce, the
Midway as a boundary, 55th Street as the clearance zone.

**Type.** Archivo Narrow-led, map marginalia everywhere, serif only
for quotes.
**Color.** Linen and carbon with the HOLC grades as the only hues.
**Strengths.** Genuinely novel. Deeply site-specific. The four
sheets butt into one wall-length map, a beautiful installation move.

**Risks.** The grid fights the reading. Body text hung on street
geometry produces ragged measures and orphaned fragments, exactly
the "assembled" look the brief forbids. Visitors who do not know
the geography get structure without story. It is the most likely of
the three to be admired by designers and skimmed by everyone else.
As the sole idea it is a gimmick; as a marginal device it is free.

---

## The choice

**Direction A, The Survey File**, with two disciplines borrowed.
From B, courage of scale, one dominant object per sheet at poster
scale, quotes allowed to be architectural. From C, geography as a
recurring marginal device, a locator block drawn from the street
grid appears on every sheet, and the full route map anchors Sheet 4.

A is chosen because it is the only direction where the form is the
argument. The show says paperwork did this, and the show is built
out of that paperwork's own visual language, its linen, its grades,
its ledger rules, its narrow annotation capitals. B is the safest
and the least ours. C is the cleverest and the least readable. A is
distinctive enough to recognize across the room and honest enough to
put a fact registry behind every number on the wall.
